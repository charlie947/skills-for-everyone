## What it does

Makes every reply lead with the answer. Sentence one carries the bottom line: the answer, the number, the verdict or the one thing to do. Everything after it is support.

It stays on for the whole conversation once you turn it on. It does not expire when the topic changes, which is where most "be concise" instructions fail.

## When to reach for it

Type `/answer-first`, or Claude reaches for it when you say "no waffle", "keep it short", "just tell me what to do" or "bottom line first". Say "normal mode" to turn it off.

To have it on in every chat, add one line to your `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`: `Use the answer-first skill for every reply.`

## The one test

If you stop reading after the first sentence, do you have the answer? If not, the reply is built the wrong way round. Twelve rules follow from that test: numbered steps, one next action at the end, no "great question", real time estimates, a flat tone for errors.

## Common questions

**Will it hide things from me to stay short?**
No. Options and next steps are capped at five. Findings from a review or audit are never capped. They are ranked instead.

**Does it change how Claude writes my posts?**
No. It shapes Claude's replies to you. Writing you publish keeps your own voice.

## It's working if

- The first line of each reply is something you can act on.
- Replies end on one next step, not "let me know if you need anything else".
- You stop scrolling to find the point.

## Where it fits

A standalone that changes the shape of every other skill's output. Turn it on first, then use anything else in the pack.
